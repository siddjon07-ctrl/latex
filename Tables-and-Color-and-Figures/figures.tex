\section{Inserting Images}
%--------------------------------

The graphicx package is used to insert images.

\begin{figure}[h!]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.4\textwidth]{mnit-logo.png}
    \caption{MNIT Jaipur Institute Logo}
    \label{fig:mnit-logo}
\end{figure}

As shown in Figure~\ref{fig:mnit-logo},
this is the logo of MNIT Jaipur.

\section{Trimming Images}
%--------------------------------

The trim option removes portions of an image.

\begin{figure}[h!]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[
        width=0.4\textwidth,
        trim=1cm 1cm 1cm 1cm,
        clip
    ]{mnit-logo.png}
    \caption{MNIT Logo after Trimming}
\end{figure}

%--------------------------------
\section{Rotating Images}
%--------------------------------

The angle option rotates an image.

\begin{figure}[h!]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[
        width=0.4\textwidth,
        angle=45
    ]{mnit-logo.png}
    \caption{MNIT Logo Rotated by 45 Degrees}
\end{figure}

%--------------------------------
\section{Minipage Environment}
%--------------------------------

The minipage environment allows us to place
text and images side by side.

\begin{center}

\begin{minipage}{0.40\textwidth}
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{mnit-logo.png}
\end{minipage}
\hfill
\begin{minipage}{0.50\textwidth}
    \textbf{MNIT Jaipur}

    Malaviya National Institute of Technology Jaipur
    is a technical institute located in Jaipur, Rajasthan.

    It offers undergraduate and postgraduate
    programmes in engineering and technology.
\end{minipage}

\end{center}
